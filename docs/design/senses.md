# Senses, and why they must compose (P30)

Author, 2026-08-03.

> *"Necesitamos los sentidos, porque a partir de los sentidos se pueden crear
> nuevos sentidos combinados, y ahí surge un mundo nuevo de recombinaciones."*

---

## 1. The unification

Two things recorded separately turn out to be one mechanism.

**D45, the floor rises** — a character decomposes into bits, a word into
characters, a sentence into words. Each level is built from the one below, and
the resolution carried at all times is one level down.

**P26, senses emerge** — the organism searches for symmetry and, when something
holds, adopts it as an instrument.

They are the same thing applied to different material. D45 composes **units**;
P26 composes **senses**. And nothing was composing senses, which is why every
finding so far has been a hand-written probe rather than something the organism
could have arrived at.

| level | what it is built from | what it produces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | bytes | units that recur and pay for themselves |
| 2 | units | roles — this one always closes, that one only lives inside |
| 3 | roles | rules — *opener followed by closer is a whole thing* |
| 4 | rules | paradigms — roots that accept each other's endings |
| 5 | paradigms | classes — paradigms that behave alike |

Each rung uses **the same faculty on the output of the rung below**. That is the
recombination the author is pointing at: a sense is not an answer, it is
**material for the next sense**.

---

## 2. What is missing in the code, exactly

**There is no representation of a sense.** Everything discovered today lives in
throwaway probes, each with its logic welded on by hand. Nothing in `src/` can
hold the sentence *"this detector detects that, it scores this against chance,
and it can be composed with another detector."*

Without that object there is no recombination — only probes an author writes one
per idea, which is the opposite of a faculty.

**D54 — A sense is a first-class thing with four parts:**

| part | why it must be there |
| --- | --- |
| **what it reads** | which level it consumes: bytes, units, roles, rules |
| **what it emits** | a labelling of that level — the material of the next |
| **what it scores** | its worth under harmony (D39), against a baseline it declares |
| **how it composes** | which senses it may take as input |

The fourth part is the one that matters. A sense that cannot be an input is a
measurement. A sense that can is an organ.

---

## 3. It settles the tie, and not by choosing

Two vocabularies were built from the same text and the referee came out split
three times — priced per piece A wins, priced with context B wins, and measured
for transfer it splits the same way. The novelty rate does not decide it either:
B has 108 units and therefore 11,664 possible pairs, most of which it met, while
A has 652 and therefore 425,000. The difference measures vocabulary size, not
productivity.

**D55 — A sense is worth what can be built on top of it.**

So the question stops being *which vocabulary describes the text more cheaply* —
a question that has now failed to separate them three times — and becomes *which
vocabulary supports the layer above*. Build roles on each, then rules on the
roles, and read the rules against held-out material.

That respects D51: it is not something either arm was optimising, and it does not
depend on which code the author chooses for reading.

## 4. Open

- **D56** — a sense scores against a baseline "it declares". Who checks the
  declared baseline is honest? A sense that picks a flattering null would pass
  its own exam. This is the failure the analogy review found first, promoted to
  a structural risk now that senses are meant to multiply.
- **D57** — senses that compose can compose combinatorially. What stops the
  organism spending its life building senses about senses? Presumably harmony:
  a rung that does not pay does not get climbed. Unverified.
- **D58** — a sense built on a sense inherits its errors. When a lower rung is
  replaced by a better one, everything above it is invalidated, and the organism
  has no way to know its own history changed units. Same shape as D44.
