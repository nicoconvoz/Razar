# The silhouette of a path (P51)

Built 2026-10-17. `rz_paths_build`, `rz_paths_recompose` in `src/lego.c`;
`razar paths [path]`.

> In the concept map "el" is joined to "perro", to "es", to "un", to "animal",
> and the silhouette of what it forms gives weight to a construction like it;
> it is taken apart and put together again in other examples where there is
> no "animal" but there is "mamífero", and so it understands — because it is a
> connected path, with pieces more important than others: some are used as
> glue in many forms, the others belong to the concept map. — the author

## What it is

- **A silhouette** is a run of 3 to 5 pieces with two of them left as holes:
  *el _ es un _*. Heard with two or more different pairs in its holes, it is a
  construction. The pairs it was put together with are its **weight**.
- **Glue and content** are not named. Every piece falls in many holes by the
  geometry of the runs alone, so it is the **share** that tells: glue stands
  fixed more often than content. In the little world *el* 0.53 and *un* 0.97
  against *perro* 0.39 and *animal* 0.38.
- **Mates**: two pieces that filled the same hole beside the same piece in the
  other hole, in two or more silhouettes. *animal* and *mamífero*, each with
  *perro* in *el _ es un _*.
- **Put together again**: a mate stands where its mate stood, so a path never
  said is built from one that was. Heaviest silhouettes first.

## In the little world

The text says the dog, the cat, the mouse and the fish are animals and
mammals, and that the horse is a mammal. Nothing says the horse is an animal.

*caballo* and *gato* are mates (they share *el _ es un mamífero* and
*el _ come _*). *gato es un animal* was said, so **caballo es un animal** is
put together, in the place of *gato* — never read, built from the shape.

On the way: with only a few words, the last letters always close a word, and
the marks of P33 took them for closing marks (*gato* read as *g + ato*). The
little world got more words, as any real text has; the weakness stays open
(D117).

## On the New Testament — not yet

Learning on the first half, judged on the second:

- **The heaviest silhouettes are glue**: *_ , _*, *_ . _*, *_ de _*,
  *_ y _*. A run of three with one fixed piece is put together with thousands
  of pairs; *el _ es un _* can never weigh like that.
- **Glue and content by share** pick out rare words on the NT (*rechinar*,
  *hipocresía*): the share is noise where a piece is seen in few silhouettes.
- **Putting together again loses to blind**: of 97,916 paths it built in words
  the second half knows, the second half says 1.18 %; with the brought piece
  swapped for another, 2.09 %. The paths it builds are glue (*"El , que"*).
