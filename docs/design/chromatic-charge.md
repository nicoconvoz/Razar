# The chromatic charge (2026-11-01)

The author's idea, recorded before it is built, with what it rests on, what is
his, what it is not, and the bar that would decide it.

Origin: *Modelo Emocional Cromático* (2022), the first document of Razar. The
author's own verdict on it: **old, and with errors.** It is kept here as the
seed, not as the specification. What follows supersedes it.

## 1. The idea, in the author's words

> «Sea o no sea un color: si lo aplicás —se llama carga cromática— dependiendo
> de mi respuesta aplicás una carga, salís de los sesgos estadísticos, explorás
> caminos laterales que nunca has recorrido y por lo tanto dejás de tener un
> techo perfecto y podés seguir creciendo, porque le agrega la cuota de
> inesperado y de paso identidad y sentimiento, aunque sea artificial.»
>
> «La cosa es que no sólo tiene que tocar la superficie, maquillar otras
> palabras para la misma respuesta. Es como un iceberg: la parte que se ve, que
> se maquilla, cambia, pero la estructura que no se ve también cambia, como un
> bloque único.»

Three claims, separable, and each one testable on its own:

1. **Where it enters.** The charge is applied *before* the answer is formed, not
   to the finished answer.
2. **What moves.** Surface and structure move together, as one block. If only
   the surface moves, the charge did not enter.
3. **What it buys.** Leaving the most probable path opens lateral ones, and from
   the unexpected comes something that reads as identity.

**The source of the charge is the exchange itself** — it is computed from what
the other said. It is not a knob set once before the run.

## 2. The seed: four axes, not four colours

The 2022 document assigned a colour to each pole of four affective axes and
mixed them. The axes are what survives; the colours were the notation:

| Axis | Poles | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| Valence | joy / sadness | positive or negative |
| Cognitive clarity | clarity / confusion | ordered or overwhelmed |
| Temporal outlook | hope / hopelessness | a future worth having, or none |
| Activation | low … high | how hard any of it presses |

Two of these (valence, activation) are the axes of Russell's circumplex and are
standard. The other two are the author's addition.

Activation carries no colour of its own: it modulates the saturation and
brightness of whatever the other three produce. That asymmetry is the right
shape and it survives the move away from colour — three axes that say *which
way*, one that says *how hard*.

### What the colour notation cannot do, declared

The 2022 document claims that every distinct combination of levels yields a
distinct colour. **It cannot.** Four dimensions do not fit in the three of a
colour, so distinct states necessarily collide — bittersweet nostalgia and plain
confusion both land on greyish brown. This is geometry, not a matter of tuning.

The charge is therefore **a vector in the four axes**, and colour is at most a
lossy way of showing it to a human. Nothing downstream may read the colour.

## 3. What this resembles in the craft, and what it is not

**It is not sampling.** Raising the temperature of a sampler changes which word
comes out without changing what is being said. That is precisely the makeup the
author rules out, and it is the cheap version this must not collapse into.

**It is close to activation steering** (also: control vectors, representation
engineering): adding a direction to the internal state while the answer is being
formed. That is the known mechanism whose observed behaviour matches the
iceberg — steer a feature and the content, the stance and the wording shift
together, because all three come from the same altered representation.

So the iceberg is not a metaphor looking for a mechanism. A mechanism with that
behaviour exists.

**What is the author's**, as far as this project can tell:

- The charge is **relational** — derived from the interlocutor's turn, moving
  turn by turn, rather than a fixed vector chosen in advance.
- It is **shaped**: a four-axis vector, not a single scalar.
- The **purpose is inverted**. Steering is normally used to force a behaviour.
  Here it is used to *break the mode* — to reach paths the default distribution
  would not take.

## 4. The bar that decides it

A charge that only rewords is makeup. The test separates the two and needs no
new instrument:

> Ask the **same question** under two different charges.
> If the **conclusion** changes, the charge entered.
> If only the **wording** changes, it did not.

A pass requires the content to differ, not the tone. Record both answers whole;
a difference asserted without the pair is not a reading.

**Control, required:** the same question twice under the *same* charge. If the
answers already differ as much as they do across charges, the instrument is
measuring noise and the comparison says nothing. The spread within a charge is
the baseline; the spread across charges has to beat it.

## 5. What is NOT claimed

- **No ceiling is removed.** A charge redistributes which paths are taken; it
  does not add paths that were not there. "Leaving the mode" is the honest
  claim; "growing without a ceiling" is not the same statement and is not
  supported.
- **Nothing is claimed about feeling.** That the output reads as having
  identity is a property of the reading, not evidence of an inner state. There
  is no instrument here that would tell the two apart, and saying so is part of
  the record.
- **Colour proves nothing.** It is notation for a human eye, and lossy. See §2.

## 6. Open (D-numbers to be assigned when it is built)

- How the charge is computed from the other's turn: which signals, and measured
  against what baseline.
- Where it is applied inside the organism, and whether Razar's structure admits
  a point where a direction can be added while an answer forms.
- Whether the four axes are the right basis, or whether the organism should grow
  its own — which would be the house rule applied to the charge itself: build
  the structure under which the axes can emerge, never declare the axes.
- The magnitude: too small is makeup, too large is noise. The number comes from
  the §4 bar, not from taste.
