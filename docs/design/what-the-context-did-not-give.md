# What the context did not already give (P29)

Two decisions, both general, both arrived at through language and neither of them
about language.

---

## D50 — A piece earns its place only where its context did not already give it

The organism was compressing, and compression alone swallowed the boundaries:
58% of the units it adopted straddled words. `lamu` + `jer` scored beautifully
and means nothing.

The reason is not a flaw in compression. It is that each piece was priced **on
its own**, so merging a junction that was already certain always looked like a
win. If `lamu` is only ever followed by `jer`, then `jer` after `lamu` costs
nothing — the context had already supplied it — and merging the two buys
precisely that nothing.

Priced **with its context**, the win disappears and the merge is refused. No
threshold, no bonus for reusability bolted on the side. Reusability stops being a
rule imposed from outside and becomes something the price already says.

**Measured, on the same material:** straddling units fell from **58.0% to 9.3%**,
a sixfold reduction, from one change in how the sequence is costed.

### It is not about language

| material | what the context already gives | what earns its place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| text | `jer` after `lamu` | `mujer`, which follows many things |
| source code | `(` after `for` | `int i=0`, reused across every loop |
| music | the note the cadence forces | a motif that survives a change of key |
| image | a pixel identical to its neighbour | an edge, which recurs across scenes |
| chess | a forced recapture | a pattern that applies in many positions |

The general statement, with no modality in it:

> **A unit is what survives the removal of its context. Information is what the
> context did not already supply.**

And the author's own image of the silence falls out of it: the boundary is
exactly where the context **stops** predicting. Not a thing with content — the
place where what came before runs out of say.

## D51 — When each organisation wins under its own measure, neither has won

Two vocabularies were built from the same text: one priced piece by piece, one
priced with context. Scored on held-out material under **both** readings:

| | priced per piece | priced with context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — 652 units | **45.1 KB** | 48.0 KB |
| B — 108 units | 53.5 KB | **44.8 KB** |

Each wins under its own objective. **That is not a tie to be broken by choosing
the more convenient number.** It is the same defect the review of the analogy
design named at the top of its list: the choice of measure *is* the verdict, so
it may not be made twice, and it may not be made after seeing the data.

There is a third row, and it is the one that matters:

| | falls exactly on a word |
| --- | --- |
| A | **29.4%** |
| B | 11.8% |

Neither organisation was optimising that. It cannot be used as the organism's
objective — that would be handing it the answer — but it is exactly the kind of
thing a tie must be broken on.

**D51 stated: a tie between two organisations is broken only by something neither
of them was optimising.** Anything else is scoring a race with the winner's own
stopwatch.

### What breaks this particular tie

The author's own test, from the morphology run: **do the pieces recombine?** A
vocabulary whose units re-enter new combinations and let the organism predict
material it has never read has found an order. One whose units only fit where
they were learned has memorised.

That is measurable without ever looking at a space, it was not the objective of
either arm, and it already discriminated once — the roots that took each other's
endings were the evidence that the morphology was a system rather than a pile of
coincidences.
