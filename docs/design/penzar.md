# PENZAR — thinking in real time (P45)

Built 2026-10-11. `rz_penzar_bite` in `src/lego.c`, `razar penzar [path] [bite]`.

> All of that Razar has to try by itself: create rules, new senses, lateral
> thinking, study the silhouettes of everything. … "razar study" is really
> THINKING IN REAL TIME: "PENZAR". — the author

## Why it had to change

Up to P44 the one choosing was Doc: "try *es*", "try *hijo*", "look at a/o".
That is declaring where to look. PENZAR is the organism thinking while it reads,
bite after bite, the way a child forms ideas while a story is read to it.

## One bite

1. **Bet, before reading.** Every hunch bets on the bite it has not read —
   "after *la* comes a word on the *a* side" — and is judged against the base
   rate of that side **in that same bite**. The bar is always unseen text.
2. **Read** the bite: the mind eats it, the lexicon grows.
3. **Think.** The key it now hears is read again (P44). It thinks **sideways
   first**: a hunch that holds lends itself to the words of its silhouette
   (P36) — each one a new hunch, tried on the next bite like any other. Then the
   markers of the key become hunches.
4. Nothing is deleted. A hunch that fails stays, and is not trusted.

A hunch holds when it has bet **at least twice** and won more often than its side
follows anything — "1 of 1" was being confirmed and lent sideways; once is an
anecdote, the same rule as everywhere else.

## What it thought

On the New Testament, 100,000 bytes a bite:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| bets on text not yet read | 5,432 |
| won | **4,029 (74%)** |
| chance would win | 2,976 (55%) |
| hunches | 699, of which 146 hold (21%) |
| **thought sideways and tried** | 76, of which **25 hold (33%)** |

What it thought sideways, in its own words:

> sideways: **esta** sits where **la** sits — maybe it marks side 0 too
> sideways: **este** sits where **un** sits — maybe it marks side 1 too
> sideways: **ese** sits where **un** sits — maybe it marks side 1 too
> sideways: **las** sits where **la** sits — maybe it marks side 0 too
> sideways: **su** sits where **la** sits — maybe it marks side 0 too

The first four are right, and **este/esta is the second key P44 missed** (D99):
thinking sideways found it from the silhouette of a key it already had. "su" is
wrong — it goes with both — and that is the point: it was thought of sideways,
bet on, and did not hold. Thought sideways means **tried, not trusted**.

On Quiroga, 20,000 bytes a bite: 326 bets, 236 won (72%) against 161 by chance;
4 hunches thought sideways, 1 holds.

## In the little world

The key world (el/la, un/una, su for both) plus "esta" with nouns that have no
twin. Its hunches beat the base rate on every bite it had not read; "la" holds;
"su" is never a hunch from the markers — it goes with both — but is thought of
sideways from a marker's silhouette, bet on, and does not hold.

## Lessons from getting it wrong

- The first sideways test supposed "esta" could only be found sideways. It was
  a marker from the first bite. What the test should ask is that what is thought
  sideways is **tried and judged**, not that it is right.
- A test world with too many words ending in *a* broke the word cut ("herm|ano"):
  the space stopped being the most uncertain atom (the fragility noted in P39).
- A hunch lent itself once, the first time it held; when a word later entered
  its silhouette, it was never looked at. Thinking sideways before reading the
  markers, in the same bite, fixed the order; lending again as the silhouette
  grows is still open.

## P46 — The family of two in the same chair (2026-10-12)

> What kind of family are dog and cat? They are "animals", "four-legged", and
> their shapes, sizes and breeds differ — although their names may be the same.
> It has to PENZAR it. — the author, answering riddle 3 (the twins on the same
> chair: "yet" and "is" share their followers and are nothing alike)

`rz_lexicon_family`; thought in `razar penzar` every bite.

Two in the same chair are **asked what they are**, with the question taught
(imitation, P34/P35) and its silhouette:

| | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **family** | what both are, said twice, that most of the others in the chair are not told — "here" is said of the dog, the cat and the table: that is the chair |
| **apart** | what only one is: barker / meower |
| **names** | who is said to be both ("rex is a dog", "rex is a cat"): an identity, not a kind (P37) |

The kin: one family (siblings), the same note in another key (P44), or only
the chair.

In the little world: dog and cat are **one family** — animal, quadruped —
apart in barker / meower, and "rex" is a name said of both, not a family. The
table sits in the same chair and is none of it: **only the chair**.

**Lesson from getting it wrong:** the family was first cut at the tall side of
one word's own answers, and "animal", shared by two, fell under it. What
separates family from chair is what the *others* in the chair are told — their
silhouettes say who they are.

### Thinking it while reading

Every bite, what catches its attention — the word that grew most in that bite,
not glue of the key — is put beside the first word of its silhouette, and it
wonders what family they are. On the NT it picks chair-mates that are truly
alike: judíos / fariseos, Pablo / Santiago, respondió / preguntó, Jesús /
Pedro, nosotros / ellos.

And it concludes, every time, **"they share only the chair"**. The NT almost
never says "los fariseos son…" or "Pablo es…": the same diagnosis as P36 — a
narrative is thin food for what things *are*. The mechanism answers the riddle
in the little world; on scripture there is nothing to answer it with yet.

## Open

- **D100** — PENZAR thinks only in keys today. The same loop — bet, read,
  think, sideways — is how instruments (P43), types (P37), concepts (P36) and
  the melody (P39) should be thought in real time: each as a hunch with its bet.
- **D101** — new senses: a silhouette that holds is a new way of looking (P30);
  a hunch that holds should be able to become a sense.
- **D102** — the first bite is thin: hunches born there ("hombre", "árbol")
  are noise that the bets then let fade. Whether to hold back until there is
  enough to judge, or let the bets do it, as now.
- **D103** — a hunch lends itself once; a silhouette grows as it reads.
- **D104** — families on the NT: the question taught is "es"; plurals answer with
  "son", pasts with "era". Whether the question's silhouette should reach them,
  or the family be read from other relations it has woven (P38).
- **D105** — a family thought has no bet yet. It could bet that what is said of
  one sibling will be said of the other.
