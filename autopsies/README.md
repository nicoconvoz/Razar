# autopsies/

The study record. One directory per session, sorted chronologically.

This is where the organism's history lives. See
[the autopsy design](../docs/design/autopsy.md).

## Status

**Nothing writes here yet.** The organism does not exist, so there is nothing to
examine.

## What lives here

- **Reports are version-controlled and kept forever.** They are the only record
  of how the organism changed. A history that was not written down cannot be
  reconstructed afterwards — the organism keeps no diary of its own.
- **Snapshots do not live here.** They are large and disposable; the report is
  what carries the meaning.

## Why the diff matters more than the number

A single autopsy is the least useful autopsy there will ever be. *1.4 million
paths* means nothing on its own. *1.4 million paths, up 11%, while effective
support fell 40%* means the organism is growing and narrowing at the same time —
which is the collapse signature the whole panel exists to catch.

Read reports in sequence, not in isolation.
